About Me

I started coding in 4th standard when my dad introduced me to Python, it started with simple stuff like hangman, number guessing game, then a Spanish-to-English translator for my aunt, a dentist in the US who needed it for her patients. That's when I realized that code could actually solve real problems.

From there I built WhatsApp and Discord bots using python and various things and then for my Science Quest I built a vertical farming setup with an self made app-controlled lighting, a Raspberry Pi car that spots ripe fruit using computer vision which provides an effecient way of farming without any human aid and a mask-detection barricade where people wearing masks were allowed to enter. An AI documentary somewhere in there sealed the deal, and I've been hooked on ML and AI ever since.

Because of my interests, I'm studying CS (AI/ML) at PESU, and I'm usually tinkering on a side project — currently an autonomous self-driving car simulated in Unity. I like ML that doesn't just stay in a notebook — I want to see it move something real.

I am also interested in astronomy, badminton, and football.
